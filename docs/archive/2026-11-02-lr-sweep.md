# LR sweep: arms, warmup, no decay

2026-11-02. Sets how PLAN C2 is settled; C2 stays open until the sweep is read.

Proposed: usui lr 1e-3, 2e-3 and 4e-3 at batch 128 (archive 2026-11-02, batch 128), one pass each over the deduped
shards 0–46 (each image seen once, archive 2026-11-01, final frame and smoke settings), warmup about 5% of the run
(the common rule, noted in PLAN C2), and a decay over the last part of the run.

Decided: the sweep adds 2e-4, to show whether the lr is massively too big, and runs with no decay for now, since cosine
is stable but too negative.

The arms are `configs/128_sweep_lr{2e-4,1e-3,2e-3,4e-3}.toml`: 15,042 steps (one pass of 1,925,416 rows at 128),
warmup 752 steps, a constant lr after it, fallback lr 1e-3, a record every 100 steps, a checkpoint and an eval grid
every 1000, the rest as the smoke config.
