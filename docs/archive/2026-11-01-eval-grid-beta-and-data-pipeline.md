# Eval grid, beta 0.99 and the data pipeline, 2026-11-01

Decided after the 2e-3 run was stopped at step 4300 and a 1000-step counter-test at beta 0.99 (FACTS, Runs at usui lr 2e-3). Each entry: what, and why.

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eval grid (PLAN D3): 16 fixed prompts, one noise from seed 42, Euler 30 steps, CFG 4, one 4×4 grid in `configs/eval_prompts.txt` order. Prompts: "cat", five ai-toolkit sample prompts without their anime quality tags, and ai-toolkit's ten default sample prompts | One seed on fixed prompts as in ai-toolkit, arranged 4×4; Euler 30 steps and CFG 4 are the defaults; the quality tags are dropped because they are just how Anima is trained. One grid, not one image per prompt, since it is well comparable. No CFG 1 column. No labels on the grid; the order is known |
| 2 | Usui beta 0.99 in the smoke config | At 2e-3, beta 0.9 held `grad_moment_cosine` near −0.4; beta 0.99 brought it to about −0.1 over 1000 steps at the same loss. Higher beta recovers cosine, so 0.99 is kept for now. A higher beta usually makes the moment stale, but with so many steps it recovers structure for the polar step to chew on. |
| 3 | Ablations run 1000 steps, not 9000 | Simple ablations do not need 9k steps. Read of the lr: overshooting gains early loss but not long term, because it forgets much more; it walks more than the signal supports. |
| 4 | Batch 64 is an ablation, at a constant number of images: 4× the steps, warmup and intervals | Batch 64 is an ablation of whether so large a batch is needed. A lower batch size also changes update intervals and step count, so the amount of images stays constant; a smaller batch may want a lower lr and could also ask for rank 0.05 again |
| 5 | Data: precompute on the GPU into one Parquet per source shard; shuffle all shards into one Parquet; training streams it block-wise, latents and embeddings together | A Parquet per shard lets shards be added later with only a reshuffle. Parquet over a raw mmap file: read in order, block by block, mmap has no advantage, and Parquet keeps one format. The CPU precompute (about 11 rows/s) slowed training to 370 img/s and would take about 50 h |
| 6 | Shards 4–46 precompute with TF32 convolutions on (the cuDNN default), the next tar downloading while one encodes | The fastest of four options: TF32 off in series (about 16 min per shard, 11 h for shards 4–46), TF32 off overlapped (about 12 min, 8.5 h), TF32 on in series (about 10 min, 7 h), TF32 on overlapped (about 6 min, 4.5 h). TF32 moves a latent by 0.25% relative against CPU fp32, against 0.17% for storing it in bf16 (FACTS, GPU precompute); shards 0–3 stay CPU fp32 |
