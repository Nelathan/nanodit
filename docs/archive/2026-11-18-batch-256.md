# The batch at 128 px stays 256

2026-11-18. Closes the 128 px part of PLAN C1.

Decided: the long run trains at batch 256 (4 × 64) at 128 px (SPEC §7), at usui lr 2e-3.

Reasons: batch 256 is fast and trains well. Batch 128 was ahead at equal images over the first 1.5M images, but the
lead shrank from 0.0168 to 0.0104 over them and had not been read over a pass; it costs 9 % in images per second,
since the Usui step runs twice per image, and it needs the full lr, since at 1.4e-3 it falls back level with 256 (logs
2026-11-18-batch-128 and 2026-11-18-batch-128-lr14). One seed each.
