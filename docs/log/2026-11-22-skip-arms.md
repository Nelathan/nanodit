## Read

- **The linear skip pays most at the clean end.** −0.013 to −0.016 at t 0.05, −0.006 at t 0.15, then about −0.002
  to −0.003 from t 0.25 to 0.85; at 0.95 −0.0003 held-out, −0.0027 Lunara. The topic's prediction, a gain at both
  ends, holds at the low end; at the high end it fails on held-out and is no larger than mid-t on Lunara.
- **c_out adds nothing.** It equals the linear skip to within 0.0015 at every t on both sets: the gates already carry
  the write's scale by t (*Hypothesis*).
- **The learned skip learns most of the fixed one,** about three quarters of `c − 1` at the ends, and scores between
  the base and the fixed skip; its gap to the fixed skip, 0.0008 on both sets, is not resolved overall. By t it is
  behind at t 0.05 (−0.0089 against −0.0133 held-out, −0.0115 against −0.0155 Lunara) and ahead at t 0.95 on Lunara.
- **The skip and the held stream add,** each on its own t: on the held stream with long skips the linear skip moves
  t 0.05 by −0.019 on both sets, against −0.013 / −0.016 on the old stream.
