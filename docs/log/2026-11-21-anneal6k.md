## Read

- The cooldown takes −0.0205 off the held-out loss (1.64 %), more than the whole second half of the pass at
  constant lr (−0.0174, step 16000 to 32173), and −0.0134 off Lunara at 256 px (1.02 %). It is twice the length of
  a1's cooldown (log 2026-11-18-anneal3k, −0.95 % on Lunara at 128 px), so the two do not compare one to one.
- As in a1's, the gain is largest at mid t and smallest at the noisiest; on Lunara the high-t gain is a third of the
  held-out one. Loss at high t moves little, but the coarse R² there rises most: the anneal tightens the composition
  the model already makes rather than changing it, which the grids show.
- The caption is used slightly more after the cooldown; the change is within what one checkpoint pair can show.
