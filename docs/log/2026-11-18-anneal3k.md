## Read

At this length a cooldown is worth about half a pass of constant-lr training in score, in the handbook's range (0.4
to 1.0 % for a 50 % linear cooldown). It sharpens; it does not fix the forms, which the sampler sets at high t
(log 2026-11-18-residual-writes), where the anneal gains least.
