## Read

σ 0.7 is not worse than σ 1 at depth 24 and one pass; its lead after 12000 (0.0012 to 0.0034) is at or below what one
seed is taken to resolve (0.003, never measured; `a1-seed42-6k` measures it at 6000). The three confounds act the same
way on both runs' every eval, so the consistent sign is the evidence, not the size.
