## Read

- Half the lr is behind at every eval on both sets. The gap closes over the constant pass, from +0.033 at 1000 to
  +0.011 at its end, and opens again in the anneal, to +0.017 held-out and +0.011 on Lunara: the anneal gives the
  higher lr more. The constant pass's end understates the gap.
- The deficit is largest at low and middle t and small at the highest: near zero on Lunara from t 0.66, +0.003 at
  the top t held-out.
- For a first pass, 2e-3 holds over 1e-3. Whether a lower lr pays from a second pass on, where the constant-lr gap
  was still closing, is not tested by this pair.
