# The decoder's gain at 256 px

`scripts/probe_decoder_bands.py lunara_eval_256_seq512.parquet 256 16` and, for the same band edges,
`scripts/probe_decoder_bands.py heldout_128_fine_flux2.parquet 128 16`: the probe of log 2026-11-22-decoder-bands with
a band edge added at 1/128 cycles per latent pixel, so periods of 64–128 latent pixels have a band of their own; a 128²
latent fills it, a 64² latent only with its lowest nonzero frequency, 1/64, so the 128 px fit is over a different band
split than the one of log 2026-11-22-decoder-gain-power-law, whose numbers and those of log 2026-11-22-decoder-bands
come from the script before the edge (`357c53f`). The two sets differ (Lunara holds only 256 px latents,
the held-out set only 128 px), so set and resolution are not separated. 16 rows and one noise draw per band; the
long-period bands hold few frequency bins, so their gains carry the most noise.

Gain, the image's mean-square change over the latent error's, by band of latent period, at error RMS 0.05 and 0.3:

| Period (latent px) | > 128 (DC) | 64–128 | 32–64 | 16–32 | 8–16 | 4–8 | 2–4 | corners |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lunara, 256 px (latent 128²), RMS 0.05 | 6.36 | 3.81 | 1.79 | 0.535 | 0.121 | 0.039 | 0.020 | 0.088 |
| held-out, 128 px (latent 64²), RMS 0.05 | 2.99 | 2.15 | 1.09 | 0.469 | 0.123 | 0.046 | 0.024 | 0.074 |
| Lunara, 256 px, RMS 0.3 | 3.14 | 2.32 | 1.32 | 0.422 | 0.119 | 0.042 | 0.022 | 0.069 |

| Latent energy share, 256 px | 0.003 | 0.001 | 0.004 | 0.018 | 0.077 | 0.184 | 0.474 | 0.238 |

| 256 px | 1.648 | factor 1.29 |

## Read

- **Below a period of 16 latent pixels the gain is the same at both sizes,** within 20 %: there it is the decoder's
  own response.
- **At longer periods the gain differs and depends on the error's size:** at 256 px it is 1.6× the 128 px gain at
  32–64 and 1.8× at 64–128 for RMS 0.05, 1.3× and 1.1× for RMS 0.3. The decoder is not linear at long periods, and
  the set, the size or the noise of the few bins there may move it; this probe does not separate them.
- **The gain keeps rising past a period of 64.** The loss weight's floor at 1/64 cycles per latent pixel (`objective.GAIN_FLOOR`)
  holds it flat there, so at 256 px and above it weighs layout periods of 64–128 as it weighs 64.
